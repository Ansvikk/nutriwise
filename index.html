This is a deterministic constraint-aware recommendation baseline, not a trained machine-learning model. Food values and prices are illustrative synthetic estimates, not a validated Indian food composition database or live market prices. No clinical outcomes have been tested. The plan is not suitable for children, pregnancy, eating disorders, or medical dietary needs. Data stays in your browser; no account or server is used.
